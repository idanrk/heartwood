Heartwood Plus

Heartwood Plus adds building your own challenges (new rules, your own order, any length or end date), a second challenge at the same time, the medium and Lock Screen widgets, a timelapse at any point, cards and videos without the wordmark, photo compare, Day 1 as the camera's outline, and three more trees. Renewal and cancelling

  • Yearly and monthly subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel.
  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account.
  • To stop a renewal, cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.
  • To manage or cancel: open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.
  • If you cancel, Heartwood Plus stays on until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

"Restore purchases" is in the paywall and in Settings, under Heartwood Plus. Use it after you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone.

If Heartwood Plus ends

Nothing you made is locked. A challenge you built keeps running to its end, and every finished tree, stump, photo and card stays yours. What you cannot do without Plus is start something new that belongs to Plus, such as another challenge of your own.

Heartwood is a tracker. It is not a medical device, it gives no medical, diet or training advice, and it makes no claims about your health. Demanding programs are not for everyone. Check with a doctor first, and stop if a challenge is not safe for you. You can make any challenge gentler before you start, and a flexible challenge can pause when you are sick or injured.
